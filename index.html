Working demonstrations

Two scenarios from the fifteen-scenario bank, running end to end on the scenario engine: a photoreal counterpart speaks to the learner on video, the learner responds, the engine scores the conversation against the IHRP performance statements, keeps the scenario record, and the facilitator validates the reflection. All people, companies and data are fictional; the likenesses are AI-generated and depict no real person.

About these pages
